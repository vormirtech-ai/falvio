The Fragrance

Amber, wood
and light

An eau de parfum that opens bright, settles into a refined heart and lingers on warm woods and amber.

How to wear

One touch of mist

Spray from a short distance onto pulse points — the neck, the inner wrists, behind the ears. Let it settle on the skin rather than rubbing it in, and allow the composition to unfold through the day.
